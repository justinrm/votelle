# music_pref

Research library v0.1. Read `docs/research/00_READ_ME.md` and documents 03, 04, 05, 06, and 08 before changing behavior.

This tree is the offline foundation: manifests, rights, identity splits, fixture audio preparation, and the dummy encoder. Do not download model weights, train, add a generator, or treat fixture tests as evidence of preference prediction.

Locked choices are in `docs/decisions/0001-foundation-choices.md`. A change to excerpt length, grouping, rights, or label meaning needs a new protocol version.
